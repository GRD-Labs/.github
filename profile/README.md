# GRD-Labs

We are a group of three Computer Science & Engineering students from **ACS** building open-source systems and applied projects in the **AI and Machine Learning** fields for public research.

### What We Do
* **Dataset Generation & Systems:** Designing scalable pipelines to collect, compress, and query massive behavioral datasets.
* **Applied AI/ML Research:** Exploring decision-making, game theory, and model training in unpredictable, imperfect-information environments.
* **Open Tooling:** Publishing our engines, datasets, and research findings for the broader developer community.
